# ChessLens scanner preview privacy

The app requests camera access when you take a board photo. The system photo picker shares the image you select. ChessLens processes the image on your iPhone and keeps the selected photo in memory. It has no account, analytics, ads, remote model, or cloud analysis. Chess positions and engine analysis are processed on your iPhone. You can copy a position to the iOS clipboard yourself. The About screen links to source code in your browser.

GitHub hosts the optional download, and AltStore Classic, AltServer and Apple handle installation and signing. ChessLens does not ask for or receive your Apple Account credentials.
